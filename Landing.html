How it works

Three steps. One parcel at a time.

1

Build your trusted circle

Invite family, roommates or your building's security desk. They accept your invite and verify once. Remove anyone, any time.

2

Tell RIVA who should receive it

Tap "Talk to RIVA" on My Orders and say who's taking today's parcel. Speak or type. RIVA confirms before anything changes.

3

They use their own code

At the door, your contact shares their own handover code. Their access ends when the parcel is delivered, or at the end of the day.
